# When do bike rentals peak?

**Capital Bikeshare · 2011–2012**

On working days, casual and registered rentals both peaked at **5 pm**.
On weekends and holidays, registered rentals peaked at **12 pm** and
casual rentals peaked two hours later, at **2 pm**.

| Rider group | Working days | Weekends and holidays |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Casual | 5 pm | 2 pm |
| Registered | 5 pm | 12 pm |

Each peak is the hour with the highest average number of rentals for
that rider group. The app lets bike-share operations planners explore
the full 24-hour pattern behind these figures.
